Introduction 
The use of AI to support the digital transformation of companies is becoming one of the main 
differentiators. The adoption of AI increased significantly within the last few years, which is changing 
the question of AI usage from why to use AI? To when and where to use AI within the business. 
The adoption of AI within an organization is not an easy task, nor is limited to a certain aspect of the 
business. The journey to transform the business to be data driven and utilize AI capabilities 
effectively is long and influenced by changing circumstances within the business. However, this 
should not be seen as a deteriorator from adopting the technology, but rather as a motivator to 
have realistic expectations of AI within the business. 
In this roadmap document, we will start with possible usage of AI technology within our business, 
then focus on suggested introductory project to start the journey that can help set the path forward 
for wider implementation. 
Business Strategy Analysis 
The print industry is faced with increased challenges especially within the last two years during the 
Covid-19 pandemic. The move into digital documentation, digital billboards, electronic 
communication, electronic books and e-readers, audio books, and environment awareness pushed 
users to reduce the usage of print significantly throughout the years. The usage decreased sharply 
during Covid-19 as people worked from home with reduced access to print services in offices, and 
the limitation of exchanging print documents physically. e.g. e-signature of documents instead of 
manual signature on printed documents.

analysis as a starting point (Porter, 1979). These forces are summarized below:

Description of the Selected Use Case for AI 
In this proposal, we will be focusing on the usage of AI in enhancing Customer Support Services.  
The use of technology in customer services have been developed throughout the years. Today, 
customer support centres operate remotely with 24x7 coverage to meet business demand. The use 
of AI to empower customer support will enable personalized, immediate, and seamless experience 
(Afshar, 2017).

With the age of connected customers, the expectations of personalized services is higher than ever 
before. Customers are consuming online services that embed AI technologies that provide seamless 
experience. When customers shift to interact with organizations that are behind in AI adoption, they 
become frustrated.  
According to Salesforce “State of Connected Customers” report, 62% of customers are open to use 
AI to enhance their experience (Salesforce, 2019).

Justification of Use Case and Alignment with Business Strategy  
The selection of customer service as the starting point of our AI journey is due to: 
1. Customer experience is a main differentiator of the business: 
As the print industry face the challenges mentioned in section 1, and the main forces are 
competition and substitute products, the business strategy within the print industry is 
focused on Differentiation.  
As Cost Leadership remains part of the overall strategy to maintain profit within a declining 
industry, the differentiation strategy would give the ability to overcome challenges of 
competition, to compete on the small market segment.

2. Customer’s lifetime in the print industry is high (3-5 years): 
As such, the customer experience is built over years of interaction with support services. It 
makes sense to focus on the highest traction surface with customers. In addition, support 
services have direct impact on customer retention and referral. 
3. Customer support is a highly process oriented part of the business: 
As support services processes and practices include logging interactions to a great detail, 
and the use of technology within the support industry is well established, there is a better 
ability to have access to useful data that could be fed to AI models. 
4. Support teams are usually technology savvy: 
This helps form cross functional teams to support the AI project. 
5. Data reliability: 
The data used for training AI models are more reliable due to the usage of digital solutions 
and IoT to collect devices metrics and availability. Which makes it less prone to human 
errors. 
Target Business Challenges to Address Using AI 
According to Salesforce research (Afshar, 2017), an excellent customer service needs to be: 
a. Personalized: customers expect a service provider to know who they are, tailor the service 
to their needs 
b. Always on/realtime: the usage of chatbots to cover 24x7 
c. Consistent: Customers expect the same level of services across the business. 
d. Omni-channel: customers expect consistent experience across communication channels. 
The main challenges in customer service that can be addressed by using AI would be: 
a. Unified and personalized customer experience: 
Access to customer data in real time through CRM, ERP and call centre ticketing system 
integration, that are analysed using AI to drive insight about each customer would enable 
unified interaction with the customer using any channel. i.e. chat, email, online portal, 
phone, and social media (Afshar, 2017). 
 
 
b. Self-service capabilities: 
Knowledge base articles and “how to” guides are continuously being enriched by customer 
support and product engineers. Using AI, the ability to process and present relevant data to 
answer a customer inquiry or guide to fix an issue becomes more possible (Afshar, 2017).  
 
c. Predictive support service: 
To excel at customer service, support services need to minimize the interaction time with 
customers and “predict” their needs. The ability to link customer recent issues or a service 
contract renewal date, or consumables levels with a customer interaction, enables the 
support services to predict the customer interaction needs.  
 
d. Predictive maintenance: 
It enables the service team to fix issues before they impact customers. Using IoT, a device is 
in continuous communication with the vendor that feeds a tremendous amount of data. 
Using AI, the data is analysed to predict device issues and prevent them before impacting

e. Customer Satisfaction: 
One of the main challenges in customer support services is the ability to collect and process 
customers feedback during and post interaction. AI engines can analyse the customer 
emotions and satisfaction using Natural Language Processing and sentiment analysis. The 
support agent can be alarmed of current or previous interactions with the customer to focus 
on the pain points. It also prompts for follow up calls to guarantee customer satisfaction is 
achieved (Dixon, 2021). 
 
Another usage of AI and automation to achieve customer satisfaction is the use in field 
service mobility. This includes workflow automation, service scheduling, technicians 
tracking, and best route selection (Boulton, 2021). 
 
f. Scale to meet customer’s demands 
With online services adoption, the ability to utilize these services extends normal business 
hours and spans countries and geographic boundaries. With the use of chat bots, support 
services can be extended to cover 24x7 services and reduce the workforce needed to 
support customers. It also enhances the efficiency of support agents by allowing them to 
focus on special issues rather than repetitive tasks (Dixon, 2021) 
 
Limitations And Challenges 
There are several challenges that needs to be addressed to have a successful adoption of AI and 
implementation of this proposed project. These challenges can be summarized as: 
1. Data Challenges: 
The main ingredient to a successful implementation of AI is the data we feed to the model, 
which reflects the significant importance and impact of data used in our implementation. 
Data related challenges could include: 
 
a. Data readiness:  
The challenge is related to the ability to collect and integrate data from different sources 
that can be usable in our model. Data used in customer support comes from different 
sources, this includes: 
i. Ticketing system 
ii. Call centre call logs 
iii. Call centre audio files 
iv. Chat log 
v. Emails 
vi. IoT data from devices (using different methods depending on the HW vendor) 
As such, the ability to collect, clean, integrate, transform, and feed data into the AI model 
becomes a challenge.

b. Data bias: 
Since not all data is automatically collected using machine data feeds, there is a risk of 
having data that is skewed, resulting in bias in different aspects. An example would be 
reporting bias;  
Since some of the data is collected through call centre logs, un-logged activities 
and incidents will result in reporting issues that does not reflect real world 
issues. Which will impact the analysis of such data, such as devices availability, 
customer satisfaction, and number of issues.  
 
c. Data cascades: 
For global organizations data is collected in different countries around the world. For a 
global provider of print services, the challenges of data collected from one region could 
impact predictions in another region. 
 
2. Ethical challenges 
Due to the black box nature of some of AI models, the ability to analyse the reason behind 
some predictions and decisions is limited. This is a challenge in systems that will facilitate 
decisions related to the future actions towards an employee or even a customer. 
 
In our use case, the objective is to enhance customers’ experience. In order to achieve this 
objective, the company could use the AI model to identify agents with highest number of 
unsatisfied customers. While unsatisfaction could be a reflection of their poor conduct, it 
could also be a reflection of other issues. One call centre practice that would impact these 
decisions for example would be the log of incidents only when a customer have unresolved 
issue, resulting in having data that reflects only issues.  
 
3. Economic challenges 
In the initial phase of our AI journey, achieving solid ROI can be a challenge. This imposes 
challenges in financing our AI initiative and justify related cost. AI projects tend to have high 
cost at the early stage, yet there are agile approaches that can be used to reduce the cost. 
 
4. Organizational challenges 
Being a global organization, local initiatives are challenged to be accepted. The alignment 
with global strategy is a must. Whilst the strategy could be aligned, local implementation 
would target smaller set of the business resulting in scalability issues across the global level.  
AI talent is another aspect of challenges, especially with the limitation of technical resources 
due to Covid restrictions. AI talent are an important part of our journey that would drive the 
successful implementation of this project.  
 
Expected benefits and ways to measure success 
In order to have a successful implementation of AI in customer service, we need to set realistic 
objectives and success criteria to measure the success of AI in the business. 
These benefits would be summarized as: 
1. Improved customer satisfaction (CSAT) 
The main objective of utilizing AI that retains customers and drive business growth. With 
increased CSAT, sales would have an increased ability to retain customers and reduce churn,

in addition to attracting additional customers through positive reviews and

As we saw in Salesforce study, customers expect the use of innovative technologies within 
their service providers. Even without having product issues, the lack of innovation is a 
concern for most customers that results in consideration of other options. 
Main KPIs to measure customer satisfaction are 
- CSAT: number of satisfied customers divided by the total number of responses 
- NPS (Net Prompter Score): Measures how likely the customer would recommend our 
services to a friend or colleague (What is Net Promoter Score (NPS)? Definition & 
Examples, n.d.) 
 
2. Reduction in Average Handling Time: 
With the usage of AI technology, issues should be resolved with reduced time as data is 
available to agents, with the AI engine providing recommendations to serve the issue. In 
addition, the enhanced usage of self-service, customers can solve issues themselves 
(uniphore, n.d.). 
 
KPI is Average Handling Time reduction by 10%. 
 
3. Reduction of Average speed of answer: 
With the use of chat-bots and automation, response to customer issues through chat, web 
portals, and even emails is faster and more relevant. The response is more than a generic 
acknowledgment response and is more related to the issue or request described by the 
customer. 
 
KPI is Average Speed of answer enhancement by 90% 
Proposed Project plan 
Implementation of AI in customer support services forms a part of the overall AI adoption journey. 
The project will be executed over multiple streams and divided to multiple implementations across 
different systems. 
These systems would be: 
1. Chat bots 
2. Customer support call centre 
3. Self-service portal 
4. Field service mobility and automation.  
The field service mobility and automation is a large implementation and will be planned separately.

Conclusion 
In conclusion, the implementation of AI in the print industry is important to maintain a competitive 
advantage and differentiate of competition. Customers are demanding more innovative solutions 
that elevate their experience, increase efficiency, and enhance availability.  
The implementation of AI could span all business units. However, it is advisable to start the journey 
with one unit and clear objectives. The project would have challenges and huge effort as it sets the 
path for future implementations. 
With the increased adoption of AI across competing businesses, it is highly advisable to implement 
this project and set the path for using the power of data and AI across the business.
