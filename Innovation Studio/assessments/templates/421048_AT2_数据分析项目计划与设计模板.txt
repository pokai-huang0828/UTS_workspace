## 管理层摘要 Executive Summary
This section briefly summarises the project's purpose, analytical solution and expected outcomes. It is probably the most important section of the report, because it is almost certain to be read. The summary can be up to one page.

## 引言 Introduction

### 业务背景 Business context
The part presents a logical and informative background regarding a specific business case/need/problem. You may consider addressing the following issues:
- what was the problem and its context,
- why was it a problem, and
- Why is the project necessary
This section will give readers a general understanding of the project's business context. A good business context may help readers quickly know the project's storyline and whether it relates to their interests. Please do not provide any technical details, solutions, and outcomes in this section.
本部分应针对具体的业务案例、需求或问题，提供合乎逻辑且信息充分的背景说明。可考虑回答以下问题：
- 业务问题是什么？该问题所处的背景是什么？
- 为什么这是一个需要解决的问题？
- 为什么有必要开展本项目？
本部分应帮助读者总体了解项目的业务背景。清晰的业务背景可以帮助读者迅速理解项目脉络，并判断项目是否与其关注领域相关。本部分不应包含技术细节、解决方案或项目成果。

### 业务目标 Business objectives
The part presents the project's business objectives, which are identified/specified from the mentioned business case/need/problem. As an assignment, you may consider 2-3 business objectives and they could be inter-connected with each other in a defined logic.
本部分说明项目的业务目标。这些目标应从前述业务案例、需求或问题中识别并明确提出。作为本评估任务的一部分，你可以考虑提出 2 至 3 项业务目标，并通过清晰的逻辑说明这些目标之间的联系。

### 分析目标和任务 Analytical goals and tasks
This part presents the project's analyrtical goals and tasks to achieve the business objectives. Generally, analytical goals are the translations of business objectives into the languages of data analytics, and analytical tasks are further specified based on analytical goals. One business objective can be linked with 1-2 analytical goals, and one analytical goal could be specified to 1-2 analytical tasks.
本部分说明为实现业务目标而设定的分析目标和任务。一般而言，分析目标是将业务目标转化为数据分析语言后的具体表述，分析任务则是在分析目标基础上进一步细化的工作。每项业务目标可以对应 1 至 2 项分析目标，每项分析目标可以进一步细化为 1 至 2 项分析任务。

## 项目方案 Project Proposal

### 数据与资源 Data and resources
This part presents the information on the data and resources used in the project. Provide the details of the data attributes and volume, and the plan to collect the data and related resources.
本部分说明项目将使用的数据和资源。请提供数据属性和数据量的详细信息，并说明收集数据及相关资源的计划。

### 分析系统架构 Analytical system architecture
This part presents the project's analytical system architecture, demonstrating the inter-connections, operations, interactions, and communications of the proposed system and its functionalities. The system architecture is usally presented in a figure.
本部分说明项目拟采用的分析系统架构，展示拟议系统及其功能之间的连接、运行、交互和通信关系。系统架构通常以图示形式呈现。

### 方法、实验与评估 Methodology, experiments, and evaluation
This part presents specific methods used in the project, including method details (e.g., algorithm descriptions and formulas, if necessary), selection criteria (e.g., why do you consider this is a feasible and effective method for this project), and any supportive materials, experimental design (e.g., how will you design a series of experiments to examine the feasibility and reliability of the proposed/selected methods), and evaluation metrics (e.g., why will you select these evaluation metrics and how will you implement them into your experiments).
本部分介绍项目中所用的具体方法，包括方法细节（例如算法描述和公式，如有必要）、选择标准（例如为什么你认为该方法对本项目可行且有效），以及任何支持材料、实验设计（例如如何设计一系列实验来检验所提出/所选方法的可行性与可靠性）和评价指标（例如为什么选择这些评价指标，以及如何在实验中实施它们）。

### 预期成果 Expected outcomes
This part presents what this project will deliver upon its completion.
本部分说明项目完成后预计交付的成果。

## 项目实施与管理 Project Implementation and Management

### 实施设计 Implementation design
This part presents the detailed design of how the tasks listed in the proposal will be implemented, including the potential development environment, libraries, tools, and/or frameworks that could be used to carry out the tasks, deployment and maintaince plan, and any activities related to the implementation of the project.
本部分详细说明项目方案中所列任务的实施设计，包括可能采用的开发环境、程序库、工具和／或框架，以及部署与维护计划和其他与项目实施相关的活动。

### 时间线与里程碑 Timeline and milestones
This part presents the time frame required to complete this project and time allocation to each of the tasks. Appropriate and feasible milestones would be extremely helpful for monitoring and managing the project progress.
本部分说明完成项目所需的时间范围，以及各项任务的时间分配。设置适当且可行的里程碑，将会有助于监测和管理项目进度。

### 风险管理 Risk management
This part presents all the risk factors that influence the project from technical to social aspects, such as risk factors related to personnel, data collection and computational power and deployment environment. More importantly, please consider promising strategies for risk management.
本部分说明可能从技术、人员和社会层面影响项目的风险因素，例如与人员、数据收集、计算能力和部署环境相关的风险。更重要的是，请提出适当且具有可行性的风险管理策略。

## 结论 Conclusions
This part concludes your project proposal by highlighting the key/unique features and advantages of your project, and may extend to some discussion related to potential benefits and practical significance of your project.
本部分总结项目方案，重点说明项目的关键或独特特征与优势，并可进一步讨论项目的潜在效益和实际意义。

使用其他报告、书籍或资料中的内容时，必须妥善注明来源。请在参考文献列表中列出相关资料，并在正文使用这些资料的位置添加文内引用。本报告须采用 UTS APA 引用格式。文内引用和参考文献是学术写作的重要组成部分。

## 附录 Appendices
Detailed material that is not essential to, but supports the information contained in the body of the report, should be placed in an appendix. Appropriate appendix materials include supporting data, detailed explanations of methodologies or procedures, derivation of formulas, conversion factors, and data collection formats, protocols, and checklists. Appendices are a powerful means of fully documenting and supporting the results of the study without cluttering the body of the report.
不属于报告正文的必要内容、但能够支持正文信息的详细材料，应放入附录。适合放入附录的材料包括支持性数据、方法或程序的详细说明、公式推导、换算系数，以及数据收集格式、规程和检查清单。附录可以在不影响正文可读性的情况下，完整记录并支持项目结果。
