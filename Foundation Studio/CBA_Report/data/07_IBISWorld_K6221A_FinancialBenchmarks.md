# IBISWorld K6221A — Financial Benchmarks(財務基準)
**來源:** IBISWorld K6221A (UTS subscription), Apr 2025
**章節:** Financial Benchmarks

---

## 關鍵基準指標

| 指標 | 銀行業 K6221A | 與整體金融保險業比較 |
|---|---|---|
| Profit Margin(利潤率) | **22.3%** | ↓ Lower than sector |
| Average Wage(平均薪資) | **A$230k** | ↑ Higher than sector |
| Largest Cost(最大成本) | Other Costs | **59.6% of Revenue** |

## Key Takeaways(IBISWorld 結論)

> **「Interest rate spread keeps banks profitable.」**
> Banks need to get more from their interest income than they spend on their interest expenses. Banks will have to manage their expenses in response to an elevated cash rate.

> **「Banks incur steep wage costs.」**
> Banks rely on a skilled workforce to provide customer and financial services to clients. While banks need a lighter branch presence these days, labour is still a costly but vital expense.

---

## 解讀

### 利潤率低於整體金融業意義
- 銀行業 22.3% 雖然絕對值高,但**低於金融保險業整體**(可能因壽險、財富管理利潤率更高)
- 反映:銀行業利潤率被「資金成本」與「監管合規成本」侵蝕

### 成本結構分析(Cost Structure)
- 最大成本:Other Costs **59.6%**(包含利息支出、IT 系統、合規等)
- 其他大宗:Wages、Depreciation、Marketing
- **意義:** 銀行業是極度「資金密集」+「合規密集」產業 → 高進入障礙的主因

### 對 CBA 房貸的意義
- CBA 的房貸毛利 ≈ 房貸收益 − 資金成本(存款利息 + 批發融資) − 信用損失 − 營運成本
- NIM(淨利息差)收窄時,**規模優勢**(CBA 21.7% 市占)能攤平固定成本 → 規模經濟是 CBA 護城河
- 平均薪資 $230k 顯示銀行屬高薪行業,**降本壓力大**(裁員、AI 替代客服)
