## 關鍵主題(IBISWorld 結論)

### Performance(行業表現)
- **「Cash rate rises challenged banks」**— RBA 大幅升息推升利息收入,但利息支出也同步擴大
- **「The era of inexpensive credit has ended」**— 疫情期低利率刺激房貸需求的時代結束
- 銀行 NIM(淨利息差)在升息週期初期擴張,後段被存款定價戰侵蝕

### Products and Markets(產品市場)
- **「High interest rates have hit home loans」**— 高利率對房貸需求衝擊明顯
- **「In the home loans segment, high interest rates have meant greater returns from existing loans」**— 既有房貸利息收入↑,新房貸萎縮
- **「steep borrowing costs are pricing some consumers out of new mortgages」**— 部分消費者被擠出新房貸市場
- **「Personal loans compete with non-traditional payments」**— BNPL 與 fintech 蠶食消費信貸
